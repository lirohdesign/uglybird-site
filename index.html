Hocket

Hocket Support

Send a bug report

In the app, open the “more” panel and choose “report a bug”. Write what happened. Turn on “include this project” if you want the project and its audio sent with the report; it helps when the problem is in one project. Then press send.

You need to be signed in to iCloud to send a report. A report carries what you wrote, the project only if you chose it, your system version, the kind of device and the Hocket version. The privacy policy says the rest.

If a report fails to send, check the connection and try again, or write to uglybirdllc@gmail.com.

Where is my work saved?

On your device, as you make it. There is no save button. The saved file is encrypted on the device. Deleting the app deletes the saved work with it.

What is a palette?

A starting point: a piece of music already built as wheels and hoops. You load one onto the canvas and then change it however you like. Hocket comes with a few, under “palettes” in the “more” panel.

Does it run on iPhone and Mac?

It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Bug reports can be sent from all three.

Does Hocket record from the microphone?

Not yet.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Hocket is made by Ugly Bird LLC, Indiana. Questions about this policy: uglybirdllc@gmail.com.

The short version

Hocket collects nothing about you. The one exception is a bug report you choose to send. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code in the app.

What stays on your device

Your work is saved on your device as you make it. The saved file is encrypted, and its key is kept in the device’s Keychain.

On iPhone and iPad, an encrypted backup of the device, or moving to a new device, carries the key, so your work comes with you. An unencrypted backup does not carry it, and the saved file in it cannot be read.

What a bug report sends

A bug report leaves your device only when you open “report a bug”, write a message and press send. Nothing is sent before that. A report carries:

  • the message you wrote
  • your project and the audio it uses, only if you turned on “include this project”
  • the version of the operating system and the kind of device (iPhone, iPad or Mac)
  • the version of Hocket

Sending needs an iCloud account signed in on the device. The report is saved to Apple’s CloudKit, in a public database in Hocket’s own container. Reports are read by Ugly Bird LLC.

CloudKit marks each report with an opaque identifier for the iCloud account that sent it. The identifier is specific to Hocket’s container. It is not your name or your email address, and Hocket does not ask for either.

We read reports to fix bugs. We do not use them for tracking, advertising or analytics, and we do not sell or share them.

Deleting a report

To have your reports deleted, send a bug report from the app asking for it, from the same iCloud account. We delete the reports that account sent, the request among them, within 60 days.

What Hocket does not do

  • It does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • It contains no third-party code.
  • It does not track you, and it does not follow you across other apps or sites.
  • It does not read your contacts, your location or your photos.

The microphone

Hocket’s settings include a microphone usage description, because a later version is meant to record audio. This version does not record and does not ask for the microphone. If a version does, this policy will say what happens to a recording before it ships.

Apple platforms

Apple may collect diagnostic and usage data through TestFlight and the App Store. That collection is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not control or receive that data in identifiable form.

Children

Hocket is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, children included, other than a bug report that person chooses to send.
